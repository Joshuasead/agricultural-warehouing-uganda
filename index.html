THE PROJECT

Better storage can create stronger farming communities.

Agricultural Warehousing is a proposed pilot initiative to help smallholder farmers store harvested grain more safely while learning practical post-harvest handling and stock-management skills.

Current stage: This initiative is at the proposal and planning stage. The pilot location, participating farmers and implementation partners will be confirmed through community consultation before activities begin.

What the pilot proposes to do

Start with a modest, practical storage demonstration that can be improved with farmers and adapted to local needs.

🏠

Prepare storage space

Prepare or rent a suitable, secure space and organise it for safe grain storage.

🌽

Store grain safely

Use raised pallets, appropriate grain bags and protective tarpaulins to keep produce off the floor and protected.

💧

Check grain moisture

Use a moisture meter and practical guidance to support safer storage decisions.

⚖️

Weigh and record

Use a weighing scale and simple inventory records to improve transparency and stock management.

🤝

Learn together

Hold farmer demonstrations on post-harvest handling, sorting, grading and storage practices.

The change we want to support

Less avoidable loss

Support better handling and storage so more harvested food remains usable and marketable.

Stronger farmer skills

Build practical knowledge in moisture checking, stock records and grain care.

Improved market readiness

Help farmers maintain produce quality and make more informed decisions about when and how to sell.

Community resilience

Explore a shared model that can be adapted and sustained by the farming community.

These are intended outcomes, not results already achieved. The pilot will need baseline information and simple monitoring to measure progress.

How we plan to work

1. Dream together

Listen to farmers and identify local storage needs and priorities.

2. Plan together

Agree on a suitable site, responsibilities, operating rules and practical training.

3. Put it into action

Prepare the space, obtain essential equipment and run demonstrations.

4. Review and celebrate

Assess what worked, share lessons and plan responsible next steps.
